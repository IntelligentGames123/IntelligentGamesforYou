Parent Guide

What your child will do

Each level retells a time an angel appears in the Bible (KJV). The angel then sends your child on a mission to help a young Nigerian, such as Tolu in Lagos, Ladi near Kano or Chidi in Enugu. Along the way your child learns a skill Nigeria needs: coding, data, AI, cybersecurity, solar power or engineering. Each level takes about 5 to 8 minutes and ends with a faith lesson, a personal benefit and a career benefit.

The game has no timers, no chat and nothing to buy. Once it has been opened, it works offline and uses very little data.

Safety lessons your child will learn

  • Never run a generator indoors or near windows. Its fumes contain carbon monoxide, which can kill.
  • Never share a PIN, an OTP or BVN details. Banks never ask for them by phone, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Check AI answers and WhatsApp "news" before believing or sharing them.

These are great conversations to have together.

How giving works

  • The game is free, and every lesson stays free. Giving never unlocks gameplay.
  • Play = Give. Once a sponsor (a church, business or foundation) has pledged, every 10 lessons players complete fund real help, such as data for students in need. Until then, the Light Goal page says plainly that no money is raised by lessons.
  • Only grown-ups can give. The Grown-ups area is behind a parent check, and children never see payment requests.
  • Your money goes to: Intelligent Games 4U Limited, through its own Paystack page (card, bank transfer or USSD). You can choose how to split your gift between "New games & free church licences" and "Data & devices for learners". Add your split to the note at checkout.
  • Where the Light Goes: we publish every naira received and what it paid for.
  • Living abroad? The Paystack page accepts international cards where the company has enabled them.

Talking about it together

  • "Angels often say 'Fear not.' What helps you feel brave?"
  • "Which career in the Career Gallery would you like to try? What subjects will you need for WAEC and JAMB?"
  • Read the Bible passage together. The reference is on every level.
