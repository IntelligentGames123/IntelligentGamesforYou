Teacher, Sunday School & Youth Leader Guide

One section per realm. Each level takes 5 to 8 minutes. Add 10 minutes of discussion to make a 20-minute session, which suits a Sunday school class, a fellowship meeting or a computer studies lesson. Print this page for your planning file.

Running a session

  1. One phone can be shared. Each learner (or pair) creates a nickname player. Progress stays on the device.
  2. Read the Bible passage aloud, or turn on read-aloud in Settings.
  3. Learners play: story, mission brief, mission, 3 challenge tiers, quiz, benefit cards.
  4. Discuss using the questions below.
  5. To keep records: Grown-ups → Export progress (CSV), which opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

The skills follow the national 3MTT programme's tracks, so the game is a good first step for older students and NYSC members who want to apply.

Realm 1: Garden & Patriarchs

The Ladder of Messages (Genesis 28). Cloud computing & networks. Learners follow Tolu's video call from phone to mast to fibre to data centre, and do data-bundle maths.
Discuss: Where was God when Jacob felt alone? How can your family save data?

The Well in the Wilderness (Genesis 21). Agritech & data maps. Learners site a borehole on Ladi's farm near Kano and work out irrigation.
Discuss: Who in our community is overlooked? How can technology help farmers?

Realm 2: Exodus & Wilderness

The Bush That Was Not Consumed (Exodus 3). Energy & safety. Learners cover the fire triangle, generator carbon-monoxide safety and solar watt-hours.
Important safety lesson: make sure every learner can say where a generator must be placed.
Discuss: What does "holy ground" mean? How does your family get power when NEPA takes light?

The Donkey Who Saw (Numbers 22). Robotics & coding. Learners program Efe's delivery robot around potholes and spot a sensor anomaly.
Discuss: When has someone warned you about something you couldn't see?

Realm 3: Judges & Kings

The Fleece Experiment (Judges 6). Scientific method & QA. Learners plan a fair test of fertiliser on Emeka's maize in Benue.
Discuss: God saw courage in Gideon first. How do we test "miracle" product adverts?

Counting the People (2 Samuel 24; 1 Chronicles 21). Data analysis & public health. Learners add census totals, compare two records, model a cholera outbreak the way NCDC analysts do, and choose a fair sample in Oyo State.
Handle with care: the two books give different census figures. The game explains that scholars suggest different groups were counted.

Realm 4: Prophets & Visions

Whom Shall I Send? (Isaiah 6). AI literacy. Learners write clear prompts, spot AI errors, and discuss bias against Nigerian accents.
Discuss: Your school's rules on AI. Why does Nigeria need its own AI builders?

The Measuring Reed (Ezekiel 40). Civil engineering & quantity surveying. Learners convert cubits, then work out area, sandcrete block counts and cost in naira for Grace's church hall in Abuja.

Skill and Understanding (Daniel 8-9). Data science & ML. Learners label training data, classify an animal and find number patterns.
Note: interpretations of the "seventy weeks" vary. The game only does the multiplication.

Realm 5: Gospels

Good Tidings, Sent (Luke 2). Software development & binary. Seun in Jos sends J-O-Y in binary.
Discuss: The good news is for "all people". How can we share it well?

Realm 6: Acts & Revelation

The Gate That Opened (Acts 12). Cybersecurity & fintech safety. Learners crack ciphers, sort PIN, OTP and BVN details into "never share", and spot a "BVN blocked" scam.
Important safety lesson: ask learners to teach this to a parent or grandparent this week.

The City Foursquare (Revelation 21). 3D, animation & game development. Learners calculate cube volume and surface area and convert stadia to km.
Discuss: What does Revelation 21:4 promise?
