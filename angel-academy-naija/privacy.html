Privacy

The short version

Angel Academy Naija collects no personal data from children or anyone else.

  • Players choose a nickname. We ask for a nickname, not a real name.
  • Progress (stars, Light points, badges, settings) is saved only on this phone or computer. It is never sent to us.
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no tracking adverts and no analytics.
  • Anonymous player count: the first time the game is opened on a device, it adds 1 to a public tally of how many devices have played, shown on the Intelligent Games 4U website. It is sent once per device, with no name, ID or cookie, and nothing about how you play. The tally is kept by GoatCounter, which does not store IP addresses or use cookies (it may note the country the request came from).
  • "Read aloud" uses the device's own voice. The text is not sent to us.
  • Giving is only possible in the Grown-ups area, behind a parent check. Payments happen on Paystack's secure page, and the game never sees or stores card or bank details. Paystack's own privacy policy applies to the checkout.

Deleting data

A grown-up can delete any player's progress in Grown-ups → Progress dashboard. Clearing the browser's site data deletes everything.

Laws we design for

We designed the game to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Child's Rights Act 2003. For players abroad, it also follows the UK and EU GDPR and the US COPPA. The game has no punishing streaks, no fake urgency and no pressure to give.
