Parent Guide

What your child will do

Crowns & Character teaches children how to choose good leaders, and how to become one, from the leaders of the Bible (KJV). Their guide is Jotham, who told the parable of the trees choosing a king (Judges 9). Each level ends with a faith lesson, a personal benefit and a leadership and career benefit.

  • How to Choose: Jethro's checklist (Exodus 18:21), Jotham's parable of the bramble (Judges 9) and "the LORD looketh on the heart" (1 Samuel 16).
  • The Jotham Line: the kings listed in Matthew 1:9-10. Uzziah grew proud, Jotham "prepared his ways", Ahaz panicked, Hezekiah opened the doors, Manasseh repented, and Josiah became king at eight.
  • Servant Leaders: choosing seven "of honest report" (Acts 6) and "whosoever of you will be chief, shall be servant of all" (Mark 10:44).

Every lesson is set in everyday Nigerian life: a class prefect election in Enugu, a cooperative treasurer in Onitsha, a water committee in Maiduguri, and more. Each level takes about 5 to 8 minutes. There are no timers, no chat and nothing to buy.

Lessons to talk about at home

  • Never sell your vote, and never accept a bribe. When your child turns 18, they can register with INEC and collect a PVC.
  • Watch out for "pressure plus secrecy". It is a warning sign in business deals and in online scams.
  • Saying sorry is a start. Real change means owning the wrong, repairing it and staying consistent.

The game does not mention or favour any political party or politician.
