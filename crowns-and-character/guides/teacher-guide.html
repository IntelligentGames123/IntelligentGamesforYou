Teacher, Sunday School & Youth Leader Guide

Ten levels of 5 to 8 minutes each. Add 10 minutes of discussion for a 20-minute session. These lessons suit civic education, CRS, Sunday school and youth fellowship.

Running a session

  1. One phone can be shared. Each learner (or pair) creates a nickname player. Progress stays on the device.
  2. Read the passage aloud, or turn on read-aloud in Settings.
  3. Learners play: story, leader brief, mission, 3 challenge tiers, quiz, benefit cards.
  4. Discuss using the questions below. For records: Grown-ups → Export progress (CSV).

The game is strictly non-partisan. Please keep class discussion about character and process, not parties or named politicians.

Realm 1: How to Choose

Jethro's Advice (Exodus 18). Delegation and the four-part checklist. Discuss: What happens when one leader does everything?

The Trees Choose a King (Judges 9). Spotting "bramble" leaders who threaten and bribe. Discuss: Why do the most useful people sometimes refuse leadership?

The Heart, Not the Height (1 Samuel 16). Character over appearance. Discuss: How can we see someone's heart before we vote?

Realm 2: The Jotham Line (Matthew 1:9-10)

Show learners the family line on the map: Uzziah → Jotham → Ahaz → Hezekiah → Manasseh → Amon → Josiah. A recurring lesson: goodness is not automatically inherited, and a family can change direction in one generation.

Uzziah (2 Chronicles 26). Pride after success; welcoming correction.

Jotham (2 Chronicles 27). "Prepared his ways": habits, records, learning from a parent's mistake. Note that he left the high places standing, so personal goodness is not the same as fixing the system.

Ahaz (Isaiah 7; 2 Kings 16). Decisions under pressure; bribery; the warning sign of pressure plus secrecy.

Hezekiah (2 Kings 18-20). Quick reform, prayer, water planning, and his blind spot: "peace and truth… in my days".

Manasseh (2 Chronicles 33). Repentance and rebuilding trust. Amon is the contrast: he "humbled not himself".

Josiah (2 Kings 22-23). Young leadership, honest workers, acting on the truth.

Realm 3: Servant Leaders

Choose Seven of Honest Report (Acts 6; Mark 10). Read a candidate's record; what to do and never do as a voter at 18. Discuss: What can young people do for their community now, before they can vote?

Please check current voter-registration details on INEC's official website before teaching them.
