Maths · Ages 7–11

Noah's Ark Builder

Build the ark from God's own plan: 300 × 50 × 30 cubits. Learn shapes, perimeter, area and volume, and find the patterns in planks, animals and the rainbow.

  • Personal: confident with measuring, shapes and patterns
  • Future: the maths behind building, design and engineering

Genesis 6:14–16 · Levels 1–2 free

STEM & careers · Ages 8+

Angel Academy Naija

Angels from the Bible bring missions set in Nigeria: save data on a video call in Lagos, site a borehole near Kano, program a robot around potholes in Warri and spot a “BVN blocked” scam.

  • Personal: stay safe online, save data, use solar safely
  • Future: coding, data, AI and cybersecurity: skills Nigeria needs

Leadership · Ages 9+

Crowns & Character

Learn to choose good leaders from Jethro's checklist, Jotham's parable of the trees, and the line of kings from Uzziah and Jotham to Josiah. Then put it into practice in a class election and as a future voter.

  • Personal: spot bribes, pressure and empty promises
  • Future: delegation, honest records and calm decisions, for any job

Why it matters

Every level ends with three rewards

  1. ✝

    A faith lesson

    A short, faithful retelling of the passage, with the reference so you can read it together.

  2. ♥

    A personal benefit

    Something useful today: saving data, spotting a scam, keeping honest records, choosing good friends.

  3. ⚙

    A career benefit

    Real Nigerian paths: WAEC and JAMB subjects, 3MTT tech tracks, ICAN, COREN, NYSC, and leadership roles at school, church and work.
