Privacy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version

Intelligent Games 4U Limited makes free learning games for children. This website and its games (Noah's Ark Builder, Angel Academy Naija and Crowns & Character) collect no personal data from children or anyone else.

  • There are no accounts, no chat, no adverts and no tracking.
  • Players use a nickname. Progress is saved only on the device and is never sent to us.
  • "Read aloud" uses the device's own voice.

Anonymous counts

We count how many people visit this website, and how many devices have played each game, so we can show player numbers and report to sponsors. Each game is counted once per device, with no name, ID or cookie, and nothing about how anyone plays. The counts are kept by GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that does not store IP addresses or use cookies. It may note the country a visit came from.

Payments and donations

Giving and unlocking Ark Builder's Level 3 are optional and happen on Paystack's secure checkout, paid to Intelligent Games 4U Limited. In the games, giving is only shown to grown-ups behind a parent check. We never see or store card or bank details. Paystack's own privacy policy applies to the checkout, and Paystack may share the payment reference, amount and the email you give it, which we use only for receipts and accounts.

Contact form

The contact form opens your own email app. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

Laws we design for

We designed the site and games to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Child's Rights Act 2003, and, for players abroad, the UK and EU GDPR and the US COPPA.
